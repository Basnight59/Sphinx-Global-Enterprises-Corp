01Who we are

SPHINX Global Enterprises Corp. ("SPHINX Global," "we," "us") is a Delaware holding company. This policy explains how we handle information collected through sphinxglobalenterprisescorp.com, including its home page and Testimonials page (the "Site").

Our ventures, including Sphinx Analysis and Sphinx Digital Marketing Agency LLC, run their own websites. When you visit those sites, their own privacy notices apply.

02What we collect

Information you give us

When you send a message through the contact form, we receive:

  • Name (required)
  • Email address (required)
  • Organization (optional)
  • Area of interest (optional), such as research collaboration or media enquiries
  • Your message, and anything you choose to include in it

Please don't include sensitive information, such as financial account numbers, health information or government ID numbers, in your message. If you write to us by email or another channel, we receive whatever you send that way too.

Information collected automatically

We don't run analytics or tracking scripts on the Site. However, as with any website, some technical data is processed when your browser loads our pages:

  • Our web host (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and basic request details (such as the page requested, browser type and time) and may log them for security and to keep the service running.
  • Google Fonts. Our typefaces load from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when you open a page.
  • Spam protection. When you submit the form, our form service runs a robot check that may use Google reCAPTCHA, which collects device and interaction data to tell people from bots.

03How we use it

We use the information you send us to:

  • read and reply to your enquiry;
  • discuss partnerships, research collaboration, licensing, funding, media requests or other matters you raise, and route your message to the right SPHINX Global venture;
  • keep a record of our correspondence; and
  • protect the Site and our inbox from spam, fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We won't send you newsletters or marketing emails unless you ask us to. We don't use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

05Cookies and tracking

The Site itself doesn't set cookies and doesn't use analytics, advertising pixels or cross-site tracking. Google reCAPTCHA may set its own cookies when the robot check appears after you submit the form.

Because we don't track visitors, there is nothing for us to switch off in response to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals. Your browser settings will still apply to the third-party services listed above.

The share buttons on the Site are plain links. Nothing is sent to Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter) or WhatsApp unless you click one, and then that platform's own privacy policy applies. "Copy link" happens entirely in your browser.

06Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • with the service providers listed above, so the Site and contact form work;
  • within SPHINX Global and its ventures, when your enquiry concerns one of them;
  • with professional advisers, such as lawyers or accountants, under a duty of confidentiality;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of SPHINX Global, our users or others; and
  • with a buyer or successor if SPHINX Global or one of its businesses is reorganized, merged or sold, subject to this policy.

07How long we keep it

We keep contact-form messages and related correspondence for as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and generally no longer than 24 months after our last exchange, unless an ongoing business relationship or a legal obligation requires us to keep them longer. You can ask us to delete your messages sooner.

Logs kept by our service providers follow their own retention schedules.

08Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information; or
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or another U.S. state with a consumer privacy law, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have additional rights, including the right to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your local data protection authority. We won't treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

To make a request, contact us as described in section 14. We may need to confirm your identity, usually by replying to the email address you used to contact us, and we aim to respond within 30 days.

Legal basis (EEA and UK visitors). We process your enquiry because it's in our legitimate interest to respond to people who contact us, or to take steps at your request before entering into an agreement with you.

09Security

The Site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections, and contact-form messages travel over HTTPS to our form service. Our inbox is protected by account security controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

10Where data is processed

SPHINX Global is based in the United States, and our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. If you contact us from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and handled in the U.S., where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

11Children

The Site is meant for adults and organizations. It isn't directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

12Other websites

The Site links to other sites, including our ventures' websites and the Human at the Gate AI policy. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of sites we don't operate, so please review their policies.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Site or the law changes. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how we use information you've already sent us, we'll take reasonable steps to let you know.

For questions about this policy or to make a privacy request, use the contact form, choose General Inquiry, and start your message with "Privacy request."
