Who We Are

Built to
Last
Generations.

SPHINX Global Enterprises Corp. was built on a foundational truth: the systems that shape human lives — education, law, technology, economic participation — have historically been constructed without the full participation of those most governed by them.

We build the holding infrastructure, frameworks, and subsidiaries to change that permanently. Not through disruption for its own sake — through documented, defensible, deployable solutions grounded in 400+ years of traceable history and anchored in the TTL Protocol™: Truth, Transparency, and Legacy.

"We build systems that transform knowledge into power — and power into progress."
Our Method — Truth
Every decision, analysis, and output across the SPHINX ecosystem is anchored to verifiable, traceable truth. We don't build on assumptions — we build on documented evidence, tested frameworks, and auditable processes.
Our Method — Transparency
Governance decisions are made visible. How we analyze, what we concluded, where we disagreed — every step is documented and attributable. Our clients and partners always know how the work was produced.
Our Method — Legacy
This work is designed to outlast its creator. Generational transmission, institutional architecture, IP protection, and succession planning are built into every subsidiary and framework from the beginning.
Our Foundation — Economic Sovereignty
True empowerment requires ownership — of intellectual property, of decision-making frameworks, of the tools being used. Every SPHINX Global venture is built to create durable control, not temporary access.

FRAMEWORK — 001

SPHINX
Method™

Strategic Parallel Human Intelligence Nexus Exchange
Multi-LLM orchestration routing complex analysis across five AI models simultaneously. Neutralizes single-model bias through variance detection, fail-closed governance, and documented human oversight.
FRAMEWORK — 002

TTL
Protocol™

Truth, Transparency, Legacy
The ethical triad governing all SPHINX ecosystem decisions. Truth-anchored outputs, transparent governance records, and legacy-oriented architecture ensure every decision serves durable human benefit.

Clinical and neuroeducational framework diagnosing intergenerational harm from epistemic erasure. Provides a diagnostic architecture for institutional recognition, therapeutic response, and policy intervention.
